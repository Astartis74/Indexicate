Frequently asked questions

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of structuring a website so AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude can find, understand and cite it accurately. It overlaps with SEO but adds signals specific to AI crawlers and answer generation, like llms.txt, structured data, and explicit AI-bot permissions in robots.txt.

Does the free GEO Checker test live AI responses?

No. The free checker runs real, server-side technical checks — crawler access, structured data, content structure, metadata, and security headers. It does not query ChatGPT, Perplexity, or any AI engine live. Testing what AI models actually say about your site is part of the paid Full GEO Audit.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

SEO focuses on ranking in classic search-engine results pages. GEO focuses on whether AI systems can read, trust and cite your content when generating an answer. Strong SEO helps, but it doesn't guarantee AI visibility — that depends on separate signals like structured data and crawler permissions.
