About

Why Indexicate exists

Google indexed the web for twenty-five years using links and keywords. AI platforms — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude — read the same pages differently: they need structured data, clear entity signals, and content that answers questions directly. Most sites were built for the old rules and have no idea they're invisible under the new ones.

Indexicate is an independent, founder-led project. It's currently run as a solo operation — not yet a registered company. That will change as the business grows, and this page will be updated when it does. In the meantime, every check the tool runs is real: no simulated data, no invented scores. You can read exactly how the checker works in the Free GEO Checker itself, or ask hello@indexicate.com anything before paying for a full audit.

What we actually do

The free checker runs 60 technical signals against your live site — crawler access per AI bot, structured data validity, content structure, metadata, and security headers. It does not query ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini on your behalf; that's a separate, manual step included in the paid Full GEO Audit, along with Schema fixes, content gap analysis, and a prioritized action plan.

A note on honesty

No one — including us — can guarantee that ChatGPT or any AI platform will recommend your business. What we can do is fix the concrete, verifiable technical gaps that make AI systems unable to read and trust your site in the first place. If a claim on this site ever looks like a guarantee of rankings or AI mentions, it's a mistake — tell us and we'll fix it.
